Section D – Long Answer
Q14. Discuss some common threats to network security.
Model Answer: Common threats to network security include viruses and malware, phishing, hacking or unauthorized access, password attacks, spyware, and denial-of-service attacks. These threats can steal information, damage systems, or interrupt network services.
Q15. Differentiate between LAN and WAN.
Model Answer: LAN (Local Area Network) connects computers within a small area such as a school, office, or home. WAN (Wide Area Network) connects networks over a large geographical area, such as between cities or countries.
Q16. Differentiate between for loop and while loop.
Model Answer: A for loop is commonly used when the number of repetitions is known or when iterating through a sequence. A while loop repeats as long as a given condition remains true.
Q17. Differentiate between Artificial Intelligence and Human Intelligence.
Model Answer: Artificial Intelligence is the ability of computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Human intelligence comes from the human brain and includes understanding, emotions, creativity, experience, and judgment.
Q18. Differentiate between AND operator and OR operator.
Model Answer: The AND operator gives a true result only when all connected conditions are true. The OR operator gives a true result when at least one connected condition is true.
